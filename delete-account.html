Catechesi — Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 2026

In the app

  1. Open Catechesi
  2. Settings → Account → Delete Account
  3. Confirm — this takes effect immediately and cannot be undone

No longer have the app installed?

Email mycatechesi@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with and request account deletion. We'll process it within a few business days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identity
  • Display name, skill rating, and streak
  • Friend connections and duel history
  • Any household (family sharing) membership or ownership
  • Question reports or proposed questions you've submitted

What's retained

Nothing tied to your identity. Deletion is immediate, not delayed or soft-deleted. Catechesi also logs a small number of anonymous usage events (app opened, trial started or converted) tied to a random identifier generated on your device — these were never linked to your account in the first place, so account deletion doesn't affect them, and they can't be traced back to you.
