Catechesi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Catechesi is a Catholic formation app. This policy explains what information Catechesi collects, why, and what it does — and does not — do with it.

The short version

  • You can use the entire app — all topics, all levels, the Daily Practice Mix, streaks — with no account and no sign-in at all.
  • Catechesi does not use advertising, ad networks, or any third-party analytics or tracking SDK. The app logs a small number of anonymous, first-party events — when the app is opened, when a free trial starts or converts, when a subscription ends — tied to a random identifier generated on your device, never to your name, email, or Apple ID. This data is never sold or shared with advertisers, and is used only to understand overall app usage.
  • Catechesi does not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

Information stored only on your device

Your progress — display name, skill rating, streak, hearts, XP, topic progress, and achievements — is stored locally on your device. On iOS, if you have iCloud enabled, this syncs across your own devices through your own private iCloud account (via Apple's CloudKit), the same way Notes or Reminders sync between your devices, and Catechesi does not have access to this data; it never leaves Apple's systems and your own device. The Android version stores this same information locally on your device and does not sync it to any personal cloud account.

Anonymous usage analytics

Catechesi logs a small number of anonymous, first-party events — app opened, a free trial started or converted, a subscription ended — so we can understand overall app usage and make better decisions about the app (for example, whether a pricing change is working). Each event is tied only to a random identifier generated on your device the first time you open the app; it is never linked to your name, email address, or Apple ID. This data is sent to our backend provider, Supabase, and is never sold or shared with advertisers. There is no third-party analytics or advertising SDK anywhere in the app, no advertising identifier, and no cross-app tracking.

Information collected if you create an account

Creating an account is optional, and is only needed to purchase a subscription or to add friends. On iOS you sign in with "Sign in with Apple"; on Android you sign in with Google. Either way, we receive:

  • An identifier your sign-in provider gives for your account with this app
  • An email address — either your real one, or (on iOS, if you choose it) the private relay address Apple generates for you
  • The display name, skill rating, and streak information described above, so it can be synced across your devices and shown to confirmed friends

This information is stored with our backend provider, Supabase. If you add a friend by sharing codes with them, your skill rating and streak become visible to that specific person only — never publicly, and never to anyone you haven't exchanged a code with.

Friend Duel

If you challenge a confirmed friend to a duel, Catechesi stores which of you started it and, for each round, whether each of you answered the three questions correctly or not. This is visible only to the two of you — never to anyone else, never a public leaderboard. If you remove that friend, this duel history is deleted along with the friendship.

If you report a question

Catechesi lets signed-in users flag a question that seems wrong. If you do, we store the question you flagged, what you wrote, and the contact information you optionally provide, so we can follow up if needed.

If you submit a question

Once your skill rating reaches 2400 or higher, you can propose a new question for the app. Doing so sends your display name, current skill rating, and the question you wrote (topic, level, prompt, explanation, and citation) to us for review. This is used only to decide whether to add the question to the app's content — it is never published under your name, and is not visible to other users.

If you redeem an OCIA parish code

Redeeming a free-pass code checks it against a list of valid codes. No personal information is submitted as part of this — just the code itself.

Purchases

Subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google Play (depending on which version of the app you use) and our subscription management provider, RevenueCat. Catechesi never sees or stores your payment or card information — Apple or Google processes all billing directly.

Family Sharing

If you subscribe to Family Premium, you can generate a code and share your subscription with up to five other people. Catechesi stores which accounts are sharing your plan. If you're the one sharing a plan, the person who owns it can see your display name and remove you from their plan at any time. If you've joined someone else's plan, Catechesi only tells you whether their subscription is still active — nothing else about them.

Children's privacy

Catechesi's core learning content requires no account and is appropriate for a general audience. Creating an account requires Sign in with Apple (iOS) or a Google Account (Android), each subject to that provider's own age requirements and protections.

Your choices

  • You can use Catechesi fully without ever creating an account.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account). This is permanent and cannot be undone.
  • You can contact us with any privacy question or request at the email below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change, and material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
